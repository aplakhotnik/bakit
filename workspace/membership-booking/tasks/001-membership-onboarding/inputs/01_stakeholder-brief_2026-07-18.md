# Fwd: Membership app — let's get moving

_From: VP Membership — 2026-07-18. Forwarded to the BA by the Product Manager with
the note "here's the brief, such as it is — can you make sense of it?"_

---

Team,

I've been saying for a year that we leave money on the table every time a customer
walks out with a pair of shoes and we never hear from them again. I want us to fix
that. The idea is simple: people **join**, they feel part of something, and they
keep coming back.

Here's roughly what's in my head:

- Anyone can become a **free member** just by signing up. No barrier — the bigger
  the base, the better.
- Members can **book sessions** — running clubs, fitting sessions, launch nights.
  That's the hook. Free members should absolutely be able to book things; that's
  what makes them feel like members on day one.
- People earn **rewards** for turning up, and they can spend those on perks. We had
  a points thing years ago — reuse whatever we can.
- There should be a **Premium** paid tier for the superfans with the best stuff.

I'd love this **live for the autumn launch**, so let's not gold-plate it. Get the
join-and-book loop working and we iterate.

One thing the board will ask: are we allowed to keep all this data on people and
market to them? I assume marketing has this covered but someone should check.

Let's move fast.

— VP Membership
