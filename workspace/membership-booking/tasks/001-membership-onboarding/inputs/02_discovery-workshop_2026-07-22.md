# Discovery workshop — membership onboarding (raw notes)

_2026-07-22. 90-minute cross-team workshop. Attendees: Product Manager (PM),
Experience Designer (Design), Retail Ops (Ops), Engineering (Eng). Notes unedited._

## What we're building

A way for people to join and book **classes** in store. (Design keeps saying
"experiences", Ops says "sessions" — we didn't land on one word.)

## Tiers — discussion

- PM: three tiers, Free / Plus / Premium.
- **Booking is a Plus perk.** The point of Plus is that you get to book; Free is
  just discovery and newsletter. If Free can book everything, why would anyone go
  Plus?
  - (Note: this contradicts the VP's brief, which says free members can book. PM to
    take back to VP.)
- Premium is paid, gets early access.

## Booking flow

- Ops: we need to know who's coming so we can manage capacity — each class has a
  fixed number of spots.
- **Guests should be able to book without making an account** — don't put a signup
  wall in front of a launch night, we'll lose people. Capture name + email at
  booking, that's enough.
- Eng: waitlist when a class is full? Parked.

## Rewards

- PM: points for attending. Redeem against… TBD. Reuse the old points engine if it
  still exists.
- Nobody in the room knew the current rules for how long points last.

## Data / consent

- PM: we need marketing consent to email people. Design: and we can't just assume
  it. Eng: where's the legal input? — **no one from Legal was in the room.** Action:
  get Legal before we design the consent step. Flagged as a blocker.

## Parked / open

- One word for the bookable thing (class vs session vs experience).
- Points expiry rules.
- Accessibility target — Design to bring numbers next time.
