# Membership & Loyalty Programme — Product Requirements (PRD)

_Legacy document from the 2025 programme. Provided "for reuse" by the VP. Undated in
the header; the footer references a 2025 planning cycle. Treat as historical — parts
are known to be out of date._

## 1. Vision

Reward loyal customers with a points-based membership and a physical **loyalty
card** presented in store to earn and redeem points.

## 2. Membership

- Customers register in store or online and receive a **physical loyalty card**.
- Card is scanned at the till to accrue points on purchases.

## 3. Points

- Customers earn 1 point per currency unit spent.
- **Points expire 12 months after they are earned.**
- Points redeemable against discounts at the till.

## 4. Events

- Members are invited to in-store **events** (the document uses "events"
  throughout).
- Event sign-up handled by store staff on a paper list.

## 5. Out of scope (2025)

- Any mobile app experience.
- Online booking.

---

> Analyst note appended 2026-07-19: the **physical card programme was discontinued**
> after 2025. The new programme is app-first with no card. Reuse the *points* concept
> only; ignore the card and till-scanning mechanics.
