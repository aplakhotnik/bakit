# Revised direction — post-steering

_From: VP Membership — 2026-07-30. Sent after the steering committee. **This
supersedes my earlier brief where they differ.**_

---

Team,

Thanks for the discovery work — it sharpened things. After steering, here's the
direction. Please treat this as the current word where it changes what I said before.

**Booking is the hook, and it's for everyone.** I heard the argument that booking
should be a Plus-only perk. We're not doing that. **Free members can book** — that's
the whole reason someone joins on day one. Plus and Premium differentiate on *earlier
booking windows, rewards, and early access to launches*, not on whether you can book
at all.

**Points stay, card goes.** Reuse the points concept from the old programme. The
physical card is dead — we're app-first. I'll leave the exact points expiry to the PM
to confirm, just make it a setting.

**New constraint from Ops:** every experience has a **fixed capacity**. Onboarding and
booking have to respect that — no overbooking. If something's full, we need a sensible
behaviour (waitlist or "full"), PM to decide.

**Consent:** the board was clear — we do this properly. Nothing ships until Legal
confirms the basis for storing member data and contacting people. Build the consent
step as an explicit opt-in, off by default, and don't assume anything.

Autumn launch still stands. Keep it lean.

— VP Membership
