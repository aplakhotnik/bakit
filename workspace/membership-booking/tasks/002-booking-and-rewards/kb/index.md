# Knowledge Base — Task: Booking & Rewards

> Task-scoped knowledge. Supplements the project KB and takes precedence where the
> two overlap.

## Summary

Scope: booking an **Experience** (respecting fixed capacity, with waitlist and
check-in) and earning/redeeming **reward points**. This task **inherits** the
project decisions from task 001 (see `../../kb/decisions.md`) — the term
"Experience" (D-1), that Free members can book (D-2), the discontinued card (D-3),
and the consent basis (D-4) — and does not re-open them.

## Inherited decisions (do not re-litigate)

- **D-1** vocabulary: Experience.
- **D-2** Free members can book.
- **D-3** no physical card.
- **D-4** consent is explicit/off-by-default; booking history is member data covered
  by the same basis.

## New to this task

- Capacity/waitlist/check-in mechanics.
- Points earning (attendance-based), redemption, and the still-open expiry window.
- Payment provider and refund window (open).
