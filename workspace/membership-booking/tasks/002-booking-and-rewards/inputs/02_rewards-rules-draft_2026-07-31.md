# Rewards rules — draft (Product Manager)

_2026-07-31. First cut of how reward points work. For BA analysis — not final._

## Earning

- A member earns **points for attending** an Experience (confirmed attendance via
  check-in, not just booking). No-shows earn nothing.
- Suggested: a flat number of points per attended Experience for launch; we can get
  cleverer later.

## Redeeming

- Points can be redeemed against **perks** — e.g. early access to a launch, a
  premium Experience slot. Exact catalogue TBD.

## Expiry

- The old programme expired points after **12 months**. In the July chat we leaned
  toward **24 months** but never confirmed it. **Please treat expiry as an open
  decision** — I don't want to hard-code the wrong number.

## Tiers and rewards

- Plus and Premium earn at a better rate and get earlier booking windows. Free still
  earns, just at the base rate. (Booking itself is open to everyone — that's already
  decided.)

## Payment

- Premium is a **paid** annual membership, and some premium Experiences may be paid.
  We have **not chosen a payment provider** yet, and the **refund/cancellation
  window** for paid items is undecided.
