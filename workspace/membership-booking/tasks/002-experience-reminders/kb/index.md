# Knowledge Base — Task: Experience Reminders

> Task-scoped knowledge. Supplements the project KB and takes precedence where the two
> overlap. Most context lives at the project level (`../../kb/`).

## Summary

Scope: remind members before a booked **Experience** (a 24-hour and a 1-hour reminder)
to reduce no-shows. This task **inherits** project decisions from task 001
(`../../kb/decisions.md`) — the term "Experience" (D-1), the member/login model (D-5),
and the consent basis (D-4) — and does not re-open them. Reminders are **service
messages** about a booking the member made, so they fall under existing consent rather
than needing a new marketing opt-in.

## Inherited decisions (do not re-litigate)

- **D-1** vocabulary: Experience.
- **D-4** consent basis: service comms about a booking are covered; marketing is separate.
- **D-5** members are logged-in with a known identity, so we can target reminders reliably.

## Likely single open question for this task

- Channel: **push-only** vs **push + email fallback** for the 24-hour reminder (raised
  in the Ops note; PM left it to the analyst).
