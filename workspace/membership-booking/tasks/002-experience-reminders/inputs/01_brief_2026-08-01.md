# Brief: Experience reminders

_From: Product Manager — 2026-08-01. Short and to the point._

We're losing spots to no-shows. People book an Experience, forget, and don't turn up —
and because capacity is fixed, that seat is wasted.

Simple fix: **remind members before their booked Experience.** I'd like two reminders:

- one **24 hours before**, and
- one **1 hour before**.

Send them as **app push notifications** — we're app-first. The reminder should show the
Experience name, time, and a link to view the booking.

Members should be able to **turn reminders off** in their settings if they don't want
them. These are service messages about something they booked, not marketing — so they
shouldn't need a separate marketing opt-in.

That's really it. Keep it lean; we can get cleverer later.
