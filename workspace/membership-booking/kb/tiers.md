# Membership Tiers

> The tier model as it firms up across tasks. Perks marked *(open)* are not yet
> settled and are decided within a task, then promoted to `decisions.md`.

| Tier | How you get it | Perks |
|------|----------------|-------|
| **Free** | Sign up with an email | Join the community; discover Experiences; **book Experiences** (confirmed in task 001 — see `decisions.md` D-2) |
| **Plus** | Free with an app account + marketing consent | Earn and redeem reward points; earlier booking window |
| **Premium** | Paid annual membership | Early access to launches; premium-only Experiences; bonus reward points |

## History

- Early inputs disagreed on whether **Free** members could book at all (the VP's
  brief said yes; the discovery workshop said booking was a Plus-tier perk). The
  revised direction (2026-07-30) settled this: **booking is the core Free-tier
  hook**. Recorded as decision **D-2**.
