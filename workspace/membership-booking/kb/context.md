# Project Context — Athlete Membership & Booking

> Durable, project-wide context captured at initiation. Downstream skills (e.g.
> `ba.specify`, `ba.analyze-docs`) should consult this before re-asking the analyst.

## Business Goals / Outcomes

- Convert one-off shoppers into returning **members** by giving them a reason to
  come back: community experiences, rewards, and early access.
- Launch a first version for the **autumn season** — deliberately lean; iterate.
- Produce a single joined-up member journey (join → book → be rewarded) instead of
  today's disconnected sign-up, booking, and loyalty tools.

## Expected Artifacts

- Requirements, user stories, and story maps per task.
- Confluence pages produced from the team's standard template for sign-off.

## Domain Terms

- **Member** — a person who has joined any tier.
- **Experience** — a bookable in-store or community activity (class, fitting,
  event). Raw inputs also call these "sessions", "classes", or "events"; the
  agreed term is **Experience** (see `glossary.md`).
- **Booking** — a member's reservation of a place at an experience.
- **Reward points** — earned for participation; redeemable against perks.
- **Consent** — the member's recorded permission for how their data is used.
- **Tier** — Free / Plus / Premium membership level (see `tiers.md`).

## Constraints

- Autumn launch is a hard date; scope is expected to flex, the date is not.
- Anything touching how member data is stored or used for marketing needs a
  **Legal / Data Privacy** sign-off before build (consent is not an assumption).
- In-store experiences are run by **Retail Ops**; capacity is finite per experience.

## Assumptions (flagged, not facts)

- Volume at launch is modest (a few stores, limited experiences) until measured.
- Members interact primarily through the mobile app; web is secondary for v1.

## Stakeholders / Roles

- **VP Membership** — sponsor; accountable for the launch decision.
- **Product Manager** — owns scope and prioritisation.
- **Experience Designer** — owns journeys and accessibility.
- **Legal / Data Privacy** — accountable for consent basis.
- **Retail Ops** — owns experience capacity and run-of-day.
