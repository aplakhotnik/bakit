# Glossary — Athlete Membership & Booking

> Canonical vocabulary for the programme. Where raw inputs use different words for
> the same thing, the **agreed term** column wins. Skills use these terms so
> artifacts read consistently no matter how messy the source material was.

## Reconciled terms

| Agreed term | Also seen in inputs as | Definition |
|-------------|------------------------|------------|
| **Experience** | session, class, event, activity | A bookable in-store or community activity (training class, product fitting, launch event). |
| **Member** | user, customer, participant | A person who has joined any membership tier. |
| **Booking** | reservation, sign-up, RSVP | A member's reserved place at an Experience. |
| **Reward points** | points, loyalty points, credits | Units earned for participation, redeemable against perks. |
| **Tier** | level, membership type, plan | Free / Plus / Premium membership level. |
| **Consent** | opt-in, permission, marketing preference | The member's recorded permission for how their data is used. |

## Notes

- "Loyalty card" appears in the 2025 legacy PRD; the physical card programme was
  **discontinued** and is out of scope. Do not carry it into new artifacts.
- Where an input's wording conflicts with this glossary, prefer the agreed term
  and note the source wording once for traceability.
