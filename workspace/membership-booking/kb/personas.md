# Personas

> Shared personas for the programme. Every task reuses these rather than inventing
> its own, so stories stay consistent across tasks. Sourced from the Experience
> Designer's discovery work.

## Maya — the new joiner (primary)

- **Age/context:** 24, runs a few times a week, follows the brand on social.
- **Goal:** join quickly and book her first running-club session without friction.
- **Pain today:** sign-up and event booking live in different places; she gives up.
- **Cares about:** speed, clarity, not being spammed after signing up.

## Deno — the returning enthusiast

- **Age/context:** 38, buys seasonally, attended a launch night once.
- **Goal:** get early access to launches and be rewarded for showing up.
- **Pain today:** no recognition for loyalty; misses launches he'd have attended.
- **Cares about:** early access, reward value, feeling like an insider.

## Priya — Retail Ops (internal)

- **Role:** runs in-store Experiences at her location.
- **Goal:** know who's coming, manage finite capacity, handle no-shows.
- **Pain today:** booking is on paper/spreadsheets; over- and under-booking.
- **Cares about:** accurate capacity, check-in, a usable run-of-day list.

## Accessibility note

The designer flagged that onboarding and booking must meet an agreed accessibility
standard. The **target WCAG level is still open** (carried as an open question in
task 001).
