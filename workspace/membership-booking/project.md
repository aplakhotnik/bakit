# Project: Athlete Membership & Booking

## Overview

Programme to turn one-off shoppers into returning members. Scope covers a
**membership and experience-booking** platform: people join a membership tier,
book in-store and community experiences (training classes, product fittings,
launch events), and earn and redeem rewards for taking part. The aim is one
joined-up member journey instead of the disconnected sign-up, booking, and
loyalty tools that exist today. See `kb/context.md` for durable business context.

## Goals

- Give members one place to join, manage their tier, and control their consent.
- Let members discover and book experiences, and reward them for showing up.
- Produce a traceable record from raw stakeholder input to review-ready
  requirements, stories, and Confluence deliverables — the same way every time.

## Stakeholders

- **VP Membership** — sponsor; wants a fast autumn launch and member growth.
- **Product Manager** — owns scope, tiers, and prioritisation across releases.
- **Experience Designer** — owns the onboarding and booking journeys and
  accessibility.
- **Legal / Data Privacy** — accountable for consent and how member data is used.
- **Retail Ops** — runs the in-store experiences members will book.
