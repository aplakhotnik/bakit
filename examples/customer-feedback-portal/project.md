# Project: Customer Feedback Portal

## Overview

The support team currently collects customer feedback through scattered channels
(email, chat transcripts, occasional surveys) and has no single place to triage
it. This project scopes a lightweight internal portal where feedback is captured,
categorised, and routed to the right product owner.

## Goals

- Give every piece of customer feedback a single home with a clear owner.
- Reduce the time between feedback arriving and it reaching a decision-maker.
- Produce a traceable record from raw feedback to the requirement it informs.

## Stakeholders

- **Head of Support** — sponsor; cares about response time and triage quality.
- **Product Owners** — consume categorised feedback to inform the roadmap.
- **Support Agents** — capture feedback; need it to be fast and low-friction.
