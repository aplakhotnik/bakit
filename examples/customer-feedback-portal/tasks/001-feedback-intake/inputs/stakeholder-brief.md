# Stakeholder brief — feedback intake (raw notes)

_Notes captured from a 30-minute call with the Head of Support. Unedited._

We get customer feedback from all over the place — support emails, the live chat,
and now and then a survey that marketing sends out. Right now an agent reads it,
maybe pastes the interesting bits into a shared spreadsheet, maybe not. Things
fall through the cracks.

What I want is somewhere an agent can drop a piece of feedback in a few seconds,
tag it (bug? feature idea? complaint?), and have it land in front of the right
product owner. Agents are busy, so it has to be fast — if it takes more than a
few clicks they won't use it.

Product owners should be able to see everything for their area in one list and
mark whether they've actioned it.

We'd love some kind of reporting later — how much feedback, how fast we respond —
but that's not day one.

Open things I don't have answers to yet:
- Do we need to capture the customer's identity, or can feedback be anonymous?
  (There may be a privacy angle here — I haven't checked with legal.)
- How do we decide which product owner owns a given category? Is that a fixed
  mapping or does someone assign it?
- Roughly how much feedback per day? I genuinely don't know — could be 10, could
  be 200.
